How this works
Prices come from the public Path of Exile trade site: instant-buyout listings of medium cluster jewels with 4 or 5 passives (uncorrupted), one listing per seller. "Typical floor" is the middle price of the 5 cheapest sellers. This page only shows a saved snapshot; it never contacts the trade site itself, so using it cannot affect anyone's trade rate limits. The snapshot is refreshed from the owner's PC, so newer prices appear after the next upload. E(chaos) is the expected number of chaos orbs to roll that exact enchantment + notable pair at the chosen item level, using poedb notable weights.
